About me

A small business owner who builds websites for small businesses.

♥

Hi, I'm Michal! Some of my happiest childhood memories are from a horse farm in England, where I had a little craft room of my own and spent hours making things just for the joy of it. That joy has stayed with me ever since. Even during my five years working in finance in New York City, my favorite parts of the job were designing presentations and planning thoughtful events.

Then came Butter Lush Bakes, my online bakery. I loved baking for people, but most customers found me through Etsy rather than my own website, and that taught me how much a simple, welcoming site matters. Now I build those sites for local businesses: one at a time, in small batches, with lots of care.

These days I split my time between New York City and Anchorage, though I'm a homebody at heart. You'll usually find me hiking, trying a new restaurant, baking for friends and family, or being supervised by Cosette, my studio cat.
