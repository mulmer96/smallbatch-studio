Hi, I'm Michal

A baker at heart, building websites for small businesses.

♥

I spent five years at a venture capital firm in New York, where I saw up close what helps a business grow. These days I split my time between New York City and Anchorage.

I also ran Butter Lush Bakes, a little bakery I sold through Etsy. I loved baking for people, but the easiest way for anyone to find me was through Etsy, not my own website. I wanted one simple place that brought customers straight to me. That's exactly what I build for local businesses now: one site at a time, in small batches, with a lot of care. (I still bake, just for family and friends.)

I spent three years of my childhood on a horse farm in England, I travel whenever I can, and I love animals of every kind. That's why the Neville Project exists. Most days, Cosette, the studio cat, supervises my work.

INGREDIENTS: thoughtful design, honest pricing, a pinch of whimsy & plenty of care.
