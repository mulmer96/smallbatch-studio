Our story

Why it's called Ray's Place

It started when Ray Seetomona, a military man from Shishmaref, fell in love with Linda in Vietnam. Linda agreed to follow him to Alaska if he brought her children too, so he did. The family flew out of Saigon on one of the last commercial flights and landed in Anchorage in 1975. Joanne was 13.

Years later, Joanne's mom told her, “Why don't you open a Vietnamese restaurant? You like to cook.” Joanne and her husband Curtis started out as Saigon Restaurant on Tudor Road, with their baby asleep in a stroller in the kitchen.

When they moved to Spenard Road, they renamed it Ray's Place, in honor of the man who brought the family here.
