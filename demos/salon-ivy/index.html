02 / Stylists

Stylists

Brian

Color · Corrective & custom

Brian began doing hair in 2005 and opened the doors to Salon Ivy in 2007. Color, corrective and custom, is one of his many passions. While running the salon, he spent over 10 years teaching color, cutting and styling in Alaska and up and down the West Coast as a technical advisor for Schwarzkopf Professional.

Kassandra

Blonde · Multidimensional color

Kass has been a stylist for 10 years, developing her style from coast to coast. She specializes in blonde work, multidimensional color and custom color, as well as smoothing treatments like the Brazilian Blowout, which requires special certification. She brings a creative, professional approach to healthy, beautiful, natural-looking color.

Barb

Nationally certified colorist

Barb has been a stylist for over 25 years and is a nationally certified colorist. She specializes in many things across the board, and is now one of the few people in Anchorage still performing perms. She's known for helping clients figure out the style that suits them best, has styled in places across the United States, and is loved by all of her regulars.

Angie

Creative cuts · Vivid colors

Angie has been a stylist for several years, specializing in creative cuts and vivid colors. She has an amazing knack for bright alternative colors and the strategic parting that goes into them, and has performed incredible transformations with just a haircut. She is incredibly kind, funny and understanding, and it's a joy to be taken care of by her.
